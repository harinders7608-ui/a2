Cookie Policy

Transparent governance, patron data protection, and artisan confectionery standards for ApricotTreat.

ApricotTreat utilizes web cookies, local storage tokens, and comparable tracking mechanisms to deliver essential website functionality and analyze aggregate visitor traffic patterns. Cookies are miniature text files placed upon your browsing hardware that allow our servers to recognize returning patrons, preserve pastry selections, and maintain seamless appointment workflows. This policy clarifies our cookie deployment methods and details your administrative controls across our online confectionery catalog.

Essential cookies are strictly necessary to enable core website navigation, enforce HTTPS encryption protocols, and support interactive custom pastry order operations. Without these fundamental system cookies, primary web services such as dessert tasting reservations and secure deposit transactions cannot function reliably. Because these operational files are essential to deliver requested digital culinary services, they do not require optional prior consent under global electronic privacy frameworks.

Analytical and performance cookies collect aggregate, anonymized metrics regarding how visitors discover and navigate our culinary treatises and visual lookbooks. We utilize Google Analytics (identifier G-0LY0HY7L01) to evaluate page response times, monitor geographic traffic trends, and identify potential navigation bottlenecks. These insights empower our technical team to improve portal presentations, enhance mobile interface speeds, and maintain stable web server availability.

We respect your digital autonomy and provide clear mechanisms to regulate cookie storage preferences across your desktop and mobile browsing devices. You may configure your internet browser settings to decline all optional cookies, notify you when files are transmitted, or delete previously stored browser cookies. Please be advised that disabling specific functional cookies may limit your ability to utilize certain interactive customization features within our online portal.

For detailed inquiries concerning our cookie governance procedures or to adjust your recorded preferences, contact our technical privacy coordinator directly. Written correspondence should be addressed to ApricotTreat Atelier, 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, or communicated directly via telephone at +1-888-777-5845. We review our digital tracking architecture regularly to ensure ongoing alignment with evolving international privacy statutes and consumer protection directives.
