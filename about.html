✦ Orchard Heritage

Artisanal Pâtisserie on Mercer Street

Founded on the foundational premise that pastry should honor the pure essence of peak-season stone fruits, ApricotTreat brings old-world orchard craftsmanship to Manhattan.

Orchard-to-Table Confectionery Art

In an era where industrial dessert manufacturing relies on artificial flavorings, high-fructose syrups, and synthetic dyes, ApricotTreat stands as a sanctuary of classical French confectionery integrity. Located in an elegant, sunlit kitchen atelier at 181 Mercer Street in New York City, our workshop functions as both an active pâtisserie laboratory and an intimate dessert tasting salon.

We celebrate the apricot as nature's most balanced culinary drupe. Originating in Central Asia and perfected across millennia in Mediterranean orchards, its high natural malic acid provides the perfect foil for sweet caramelized butter pastry and rich almond marzipan. By utilizing antique French copper cauldrons and slow lamination techniques, we create confections that honor the authentic taste of the orchard.

"We do not mask the apricot behind heavy sugar. We elevate its natural tart acidity through precise thermal caramelization."

Chef Laurent Mercier • Master Confiturier
Provenance Standards

Heirloom Stone Fruit Provenance

Complete transparency from century-old family orchards to the finished plated dessert.

California Blenheim Apricots

Grown in the microclimates of the Santa Clara and San Benito valleys, these rare heritage apricots feature sweet-tart aromatics that supermarket cultivars cannot rival.

Turkish Sun-Dried Malatya

Naturally dehydrated under the scorching sun of Eastern Anatolia, these golden stone fruits concentrate natural fruit sugars into a rich, chewy confectionery center.

Copper Cauldron Cooking

Simmered in solid copper pans that distribute BTUs uniformly, flash-boiling moisture away in minutes to retain vibrant beta-carotene and floral fruit esters.
